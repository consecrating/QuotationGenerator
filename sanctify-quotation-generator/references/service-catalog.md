## C. HOW TO PRESENT PRICING (formatting rules)

- Currency symbol: **₹** (rupee). Format like `₹3,200/-`, `₹8,500/year`,
  `₹26,010/- (One Time Charge)`.
- Each pricing section = one table with **Sr. No. | Services | Quantity | Pricing**
  and a bold **Total** row.
- Recompute every Total; then the grand total block = **Subtotal → GST 18% → Grand Total**.
- If a quote mixes one-time + monthly + yearly, keep them in separate tables with
  their own totals (don't add ₹/month to ₹/year). The grand-total block then
  summarises the *comparable* one-time figure and lists recurring items separately —
  ask the staff how they want it summarised if unsure.
