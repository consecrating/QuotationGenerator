# Sanctify Industry Playbooks

Generic proposals lose. These playbooks let a quotation open with a paragraph that
proves Sanctify understands *that client's* specific market — written for Goa.

**How the AI uses this:** once the client's industry is known, pull the matching
block and use it for the proposal's strategy narrative, the recommended service mix,
and the objection the client is most likely to raise on the call.

> Describe **capability and method**, never guaranteed outcomes. "Designed to increase
> qualified enquiries" — never "will get you 50 leads."

---

## Real Estate / Builders

**Market reality:** Buyers research online for weeks before making contact. Trust and
project credibility decide the enquiry. Competition in Vasco, Panjim and North Goa is
heavily digital, and a single sale is high-value — so cost-per-lead tolerance is high.

**Opening narrative:**
> The real estate market in Goa is highly competitive and digitally influenced. Buyers
> search extensively online before making property decisions, so a premium digital
> presence directly improves trust, visibility, enquiry quality, and conversions. This
> proposal focuses on project positioning, qualified lead generation, and long-term
> brand authority.

**Recommended mix:** Static creatives (project showcase) → Facebook + Instagram lead
campaigns → Google Ads on high-intent search → Google Maps/locality SEO → landing page.

**What moves the needle:** locality-level targeting (Vasco, Dabolim, Chicalim),
project-showcase creatives, walkthrough video, enquiry forms that capture budget and
possession timeline.

**Likely objection:** *"Can you guarantee X leads?"*
**Answer:** No agency can guarantee lead volume. What we commit to is targeting
precision, creative quality, campaign optimisation, and transparent reporting — and a
7-day test window to establish a realistic cost-per-enquiry before scaling.

## Hospitality / Resorts / Restaurants

**Market reality:** Extremely seasonal (peak Nov–Feb), OTA-dependent, and driven by
visual desire plus social proof. Direct bookings are far more profitable than OTA
bookings, so shifting share to direct is the real commercial win.

**Opening narrative:**
> Goa's hospitality market is seasonal, visually driven, and intensely competitive.
> Guests decide from imagery, reviews, and social proof long before they book. This
> proposal builds a premium visual presence, strengthens direct-booking demand, and
> reduces dependence on third-party platforms.

**Recommended mix:** High-quality reels/photo content → Instagram-led presence →
Google Maps + review management → seasonal campaign bursts before peak → direct-booking
landing page.

**What moves the needle:** reels over static, user-generated content, Google Business
Profile with fresh photos, campaigns timed 6–8 weeks *ahead* of season.

**Likely objection:** *"We already get OTA bookings."*
**Answer:** OTAs charge 15–25% commission. Every booking moved to direct is margin
recovered — the objective is shifting share, not replacing OTAs.

## Healthcare / Clinics / Hospitals

**Market reality:** Trust-led and heavily local ("near me" search). Advertising is
regulated and sensitive — claims must stay conservative and ethical.

**Opening narrative:**
> Healthcare decisions are driven by trust, proximity, and credibility. Patients search
> locally and evaluate reputation carefully. This proposal focuses on building
> authority, local search visibility, and clear, ethical communication of services.

**Recommended mix:** Local SEO + Google Maps (dominant channel) → doctor/specialty
credibility content → review management → restrained awareness campaigns.

**What moves the needle:** "near me" local SEO, complete and accurate Maps listing,
doctor profiles, patient education content, fast review response.

**Care required:** no outcome or cure claims, no patient imagery/testimonials without
written consent, comply with medical advertising norms. **Flag ad copy for the client's
medical lead to approve.**

**Likely objection:** *"Is advertising appropriate for us?"*
**Answer:** The work is credibility and accessibility — being findable and clearly
informative — not persuasion. Everything is framed as patient education.

## Education / Schools / Preschools

**Market reality:** Sharply cyclical around admission season, with parents (not
students) as the decision-makers. Reputation and word-of-mouth dominate.

**Opening narrative:**
> Admission decisions are made by parents who evaluate credibility, facilities, safety,
> and outcomes. This proposal focuses on admission-season visibility, parent-focused
> communication, and building long-term institutional reputation.

**Recommended mix:** Admission-cycle campaign bursts → facility/faculty/activity
creatives → parent-targeted local campaigns → enquiry-capture landing page → Maps.

**What moves the needline:** start **8–10 weeks before** admissions open, answer parent
questions directly in creatives (fees, curriculum, safety, transport), show real campus
and activity photography, capture enquiries with phone follow-up.

**Likely objection:** *"We fill seats by word-of-mouth."*
**Answer:** Word-of-mouth still starts with a search. Parents verify online before
visiting — the goal is that what they find matches the reputation you already have.

## Automobile / Dealerships

**Market reality:** Long consideration cycle, high ticket value, brand guidelines
usually imposed by the OEM. Test drives and offers are the conversion events.

**Opening narrative:**
> Automobile purchases involve extended research and comparison. Buyers evaluate
> models, offers, and dealership credibility online before visiting a showroom. This
> proposal focuses on model-level visibility, test-drive lead generation, and premium
> brand presentation consistent with manufacturer standards.

**Recommended mix:** Model-specific creatives → test-drive lead campaigns → Google Ads
on model/comparison search → offer-led seasonal bursts → service-retention campaigns.

**What moves the needle:** test-drive as the primary CTA, model-level (not generic)
targeting, festive/offer timing, and remembering that **service and accessories** are a
recurring revenue stream worth its own campaigns.

**Note:** always confirm OEM co-op/brand guideline constraints before finalising creative.

## Retail / F&B / Local Business

**Market reality:** Footfall-driven, small radius, price and freshness sensitive. Maps
and Instagram do most of the work.

**Opening narrative:**
> Local retail success depends on visibility at the moment of intent. Customers search
> nearby, compare quickly, and decide fast. This proposal focuses on local discovery,
> consistent presence, and offer-led conversion within your catchment area.

**Recommended mix:** Google Maps optimisation → hyper-local radius campaigns →
Instagram presence → offer/festival bursts → WhatsApp-led conversion.

**What moves the needle:** Maps listing with current photos and hours, 3–5 km radius
targeting, festival/weekend offers, WhatsApp as the conversion channel.

## Cross-industry structure for any proposal

1. **Market reality** — prove you understand their specific competitive pressure.
2. **Strategic focus areas** — 6–8 bullets naming the actual levers.
3. **Proposed services** — service → what it achieves (not a feature list).
4. **Commercial proposal** — the priced table from the engine.
5. **Why Sanctify** — positioning, not a brag list.
6. **Close** — partnership framing plus a clear next step.

**The differentiator:** most agencies send a price list. A Sanctify proposal opens by
demonstrating it understands the client's market, *then* prices the work.
